Fidget: Mazes of Boreternia
Support

Help for Fidget on iPhone and iPad.

Get in touch

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue at github.com/averack/fidget-privacy/issues and say which device you play on. We read every one.

How do I play?

Drag on the see-through thumb pad to walk. Your lantern lights up the maze as you go; find the exit hidden in the fog to reach the next maze. You can move the pad to the left, right or middle, or let it follow your thumb, in Settings.

I bought Remove ads. How do I get it on another device?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases under Support Fidget, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Will I lose my progress on a new device?

Your lifetime progress (best maze, mazes cleared, trinkets and similar stats) is backed up to your own iCloud account and comes back on a new device signed in to the same Apple Account. The run you're in the middle of stays on the device you're playing on.

Game Center

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings to use the leaderboards (Best maze and the Daily Maze) and achievements. The game works fine without it.
